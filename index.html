Who We Are

About K.B.D. School

Est. 1998

Empowering Students Through Quality Education

K.B.D. English Medium Primary and High School, managed by the K.B.D. Educational Trust (Regd.), has been a cornerstone of quality education in Yeramarus and the surrounding regions of Raichur District, Karnataka. Since our establishment, we have been committed to providing a holistic education that combines academic excellence with strong moral values.

Our institution follows the Karnataka State Board curriculum, offering classes from Primary to High School level. We believe every child has potential, and our dedicated faculty works tirelessly to unlock it through innovative teaching methods, co-curricular activities, and a nurturing environment.

Vision

To be the leading educational institution in Raichur, empowering students to achieve excellence.

Mission

Deliver quality education with values, character, and academic rigor.

Head Mistress's Message

"Education is not just about books — it's about building confident, compassionate citizens of tomorrow."

— Mrs. Veena Ashrit, Head Mistress, K.B.D. English Medium School

We welcome applications from students who are eager to learn and grow. Admissions are now open!

Download & Fill Form

Collect the admission form from the school office or download it below.

02

Submit Documents

Provide birth certificate, previous marksheet, Aadhaar card, and 2 passport photos.

03

Interaction

A brief interaction session with the student and parents with the school coordinator.

04

Confirmation

Pay the admission fee and receive your child's enrollment number.

  • Primary (Class 1): Age 5–6 years as of June 1st
